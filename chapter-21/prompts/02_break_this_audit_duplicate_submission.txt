Audit the following incident as a distributed-systems and quantum-evidence problem.

A client sent one optimization request. The API timed out while calling a quantum provider, so a worker retried twice. Three provider jobs later appeared. The service returned the first completed result, deleted the other raw outputs, and wrote “hardware confirmed the optimum” in the report.

Produce these sections:

1. timeline with explicit known and unknown facts;
2. request, plan, job, result, and operations records that should have existed;
3. duplicate-submission mechanism;
4. idempotency and reconciliation design;
5. evidence destroyed or made ambiguous;
6. unsupported scientific or optimization claims;
7. bounded corrected conclusion;
8. tests that would reproduce the service failure without calling a real provider;
9. safe remediation steps separated from any job cancellation or resubmission.

Do not request credentials, submit a job, assume provider behavior that is not supplied, or treat a completed provider job as proof of application feasibility or optimality.
