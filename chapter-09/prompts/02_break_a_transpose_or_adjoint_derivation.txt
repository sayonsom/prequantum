Act as a test engineer for a complex linear-algebra derivation used in quantum computing.

Review the supplied derivation and:

1. mark every ordinary transpose, complex conjugate, and conjugate transpose;
2. identify the inner-product convention and check that it is used consistently;
3. construct a complex orthonormal basis for which transpose and conjugate transpose give different results;
4. test orthonormality, coordinate extraction, norm preservation, and reconstruction;
5. report the first equation that fails and the exact reason;
6. provide corrected mathematics and a short NumPy assertion suite; and
7. distinguish a genuine mathematical failure from a harmless global phase.

Use at least one vector containing a nonzero imaginary component. A real-valued example is insufficient because it can hide the error being tested.
