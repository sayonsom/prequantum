Audit the following claim: "A BB84 simulation produced zero errors, so the
resulting key is unconditionally secure." Build a claim-evidence table. Check
the channel model, authenticated classical channel, sifting, parameter
estimation, error correction, privacy amplification, finite-key effects,
device assumptions, side channels, composable security definition, and key
handling. Identify what a prepare-and-measure simulation can demonstrate and
what it cannot demonstrate. Propose the smallest corrected experiment and a
literal conclusion that the evidence supports. Do not describe zero QBER as
proof that no adversary exists.
